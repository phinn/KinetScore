## 简介description(≤4000)

### en
Sheet music that opens like a book.

KinetScore turns iPhone Duo into a two-page score stand. Import your PDF scores and read them across the full unfolded display — page turns, half-open practice mode, and a metronome built in.

TWO-PAGE SPREAD
Unfold and see facing pages side by side, exactly like a printed score. Turn pages with a tap.

HALF-OPEN PRACTICE MODE
Fold the screen halfway: the score stays up top, controls stay at the bottom. Your page never flips by accident while you play.

BUILT-IN METRONOME
Start the beat without leaving your score. Adjust tempo in one tap on the console bar.

COVER BOOKMARK
Close the phone and a live bookmark card shows your score, current page and tempo — reopen and continue instantly.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything stays on your device. No account, no tracking, no cloud.

Free to start with 5 scores and a limited metronome. One-time unlock for everything.

## 审核备注 Review Notes(4.3 风险对策)
KinetScore is a PDF sheet-music reader purpose-built for iPhone Duo's dual-display postures (folded / half-open / unfolded). It is functionally distinct from template apps: score library, spread pagination, hinge-aware console and SceneAccessory bookmark are custom-built. Demo account not required — app ships with 5 public-domain sample scores and supports user-imported PDFs via Files.
